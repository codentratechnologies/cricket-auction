We've Got Answers!

Everything you need to know about CricSquad
and running successful cricket auctions.

What is CricSquad?

CricSquad is an all-in-one platform for cricket organizers to create and manage auctions, add teams and players, go live, and build winning squads with ease.

How do I create an auction?

Simply register as an organizer, click "Create Auction", fill in your tournament details, set squad limits, and invite team owners!

Can I invite multiple teams to my auction?

Yes! Depending on your plan, you can invite anywhere from 2 to 16+ teams to participate in real-time bidding.

How does live auction work?

Our real-time engine syncs bids across all connected devices instantly, showing live timers, player stats, and remaining budgets.

What payment methods are available?

We support all major payment options including Credit/Debit cards, Net Banking, UPI, and Digital Wallets.

Is my data safe and secure?

Absolutely. We use enterprise-grade SSL encryption and secure cloud servers to keep all your auction data and player information safe.

Where can I get help if I face an issue?

Our dedicated support team is available 24/7 via Live Chat, Email, and Phone support.
